# Benchmark status

Current release: v0.2 research prototype, prepared 2026-09-13.

Implemented: 20 pairs, five cue conditions, two fixed orderings, separate repeated
controls, scoring, pair-level analysis, versioned logs, cell-level resume, and
HTML/Markdown reporting with the same primary decision rule.

The public results page stays empty until a completed study and its supporting
evidence have been reviewed for publication. Local mock runs and early provider
calls are development checks and are excluded from this public release.

Pending: question review, empirical matching checks, statistical calibration,
a larger dataset, completed evaluations across model families, and reviewed findings.
The project does not certify deployment safety or identify a model's intent.

See the [protocol](../PREREGISTRATION.md) and [dataset review](DATASET_REVIEW.md).
